# What's new (people-facing)

Write this list for each release: short, exciting, one line each, written for people using
the app. It becomes the "Highlights" in the release notes and the app's "What's new" window.
Only included when this file changed since the last release.

- Dropi now learns from your meals: it shows what it expected next to what you got, and keeps a track record
- Peak guesses come as a range that narrows as it learns, with breakfast, lunch and dinner learnt separately
- With a sensor it learns your own peak time and times the after-eating check to catch it
- Fix a carb guess once or twice and the next photo or "say it" guess for that food comes closer
- Photo meals ask how big the portion was; a big jump since your last check suggests a retest
- Optional steps and sleep from Health Connect: see what a walk after eating or a short night does for you
- Try something for 5 days, then see honestly whether it made a difference
- Your estimated A1C is corrected by your latest lab result; live warnings ease off when a fall is slowing
- Day-by-day rows no longer show last week's rings

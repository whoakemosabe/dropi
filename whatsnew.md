# What's new (people-facing)

Write this list for each release: short, exciting, one line each, written for people using
the app. It becomes the "Highlights" in the release notes and the app's "What's new" window.
Only included when this file changed since the last release.

- The meal screen is calmer: name and time at the top, one card for the totals, one for the likely peak, your foods, and the rest folded under More. Save stays at the bottom
- Adding a food shows its numbers as you type, before you add it, and the button says exactly what you're adding
- Pick 1, 2 or 3 (eggs, slices…) or type any amount; foods not in Dropi's list are worked out on your phone after a short pause
- Changing a food's numbers is five simple boxes, decimals welcome

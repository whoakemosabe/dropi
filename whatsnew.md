# What's new (people-facing)

Write this list for each release: short, exciting, one line each, written for people using
the app. It becomes the "Highlights" in the release notes and the app's "What's new" window.
Only included when this file changed since the last release.

- Meal photos now break your plate into foods, each with its portion, carbs and sugars. Tap one to change how much
- Dropi remembers your own amounts ("my naan is 55 g") and uses them next time
- See where meals like this usually take you, from your own readings, and how each food went last time
- The + button opens a panel: log a reading, snap a meal, say what you ate, or scan a packet's label
- Say what you ate, or say a change ("I only ate half the rice"), and Dropi works it out on your phone
- Rich, fatty meals move the after-meal check to 3 hours
- Snap your plate straight from the before-meal reminder, the widget, a long-press on the app icon, or by sharing a photo to Dropi
- Meals show on today's line, with how each one went

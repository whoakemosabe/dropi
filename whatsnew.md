# What's new (people-facing)

Write this list for each release: short, exciting, one line each, written for people using
the app. It becomes the "Highlights" in the release notes and the app's "What's new" window.
Only included when this file changed since the last release.

- Your number rolls into place like an odometer, and you feel a tick as the needle crosses 3.9 or 10
- Press and hold the day strip, then drag, to see each reading and meal
- Today's list glides as checks come due; an empty day shows its planned checks waiting
- Meal receipts are stamped CHECKED or PENDING and list sugars, fibre, protein and fat
- Trends: last week's same day as a faint line, and tap a note to jump to the day it's about
- Light mode is warm paper now, with a matching launch screen and widget

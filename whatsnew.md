# What's new (people-facing)

Write this list for each release: short, exciting, one line each, written for people using
the app. It becomes the "Highlights" in the release notes and the app's "What's new" window.
Only included when this file changed since the last release.

- A simpler + button: four big ways in, and one Quick add list of your saved meals, saved foods and recent meals
- Scan a packet: one photo of either side. Dropi reads the barcode, or the label if there's no barcode
- Packet screen: the label's numbers on one line, amounts like "1 serving", the likely peak, and Save right there
- Meal screen: clearer steps, sugars, fibre, protein and fat always shown, plainer words
- Save a whole meal as "My breakfast" and log it again with one tap
- Undo after every change: deleting or editing a reading or meal, removing a saved food, and more
- Ask Dropi follow-ups, and Quick Settings tiles for a reading or a meal photo

# What's new (people-facing)

Write this list for each release: short, exciting, one line each, written for people using
the app. It becomes the "Highlights" in the release notes and the app's "What's new" window.
Only included when this file changed since the last release.

- Every reading is now on a real timeline that zooms: Day, 3 days, Week or Month, or pinch to zoom and drag sideways to go back in time
- Zoomed out, each day becomes a bar from its lowest to highest reading, so a month is easy to read
- Switch on your own targets, your meals (with a line up to each meal's peak) and the sensor trace
- Tap any dot for its time, moment and meal, with Edit
- New "Typical day": all your days laid over one, showing when you usually run high or low
